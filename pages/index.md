I am a PhD candidate at the [Chair in Mathematical Optimization](https://www.wim.uni-mannheim.de/staudigl/) at Mannheim University, supervised by [Prof. Mathias Staudigl](mailto:mathias.staudigl@uni-mannheim.de).

My research is about algorithms for hierarchical and bilevel variational inequalities — problems where a system of interacting agents first reaches an equilibrium, and then a planner selects among equilibria according to some criterion. This two-level structure appears naturally in energy markets, transport networks, and adversarial learning. I develop stochastic extragradient and operator splitting methods with convergence guarantees for these problems, both in Euclidean and Bregman settings, and implement them in Python.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a working student in energy trading and portfolio management (BayWa r.e., MVV Trading), where I built electricity price forecasting models and optimized battery storage dispatch.


## Research Interests
- Hierarchical and bilevel variational inequalities
- Stochastic extragradient and variance reduction methods
- Operator splitting and Bregman methods
- Nash and generalized Nash equilibrium problems
- Applications in energy systems and machine learning
